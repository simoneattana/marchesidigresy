La Serra

Total area: 11.01.06 Ha

Located in Cassine, Monferrato, La Serra estate consists of 11 hectares of vineyards with an ideal southern exposure, at an altitude that varies from 230 to 340 meters.

Its position near the Bormida river, the fertility of the soil which is a mixture of clay and limestone, as well as the mild winds blowing from Liguria: every element of the microclimate intensifies the aromatic notes of the grapes cultivated here.

Varieties grown here: Barbera, Moscato, Merlot.
