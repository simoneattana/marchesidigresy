Monte Aribaldo

Total area: 10.06.09 Ha

Just two kilometers away from Martinenga, Monte Aribaldo rises in the municipality of Treiso d'Alba, and dominates the surrounding hills from a height, offering a breathtaking viewpoint on the whole region.

Here, marquis Carlo di Grésy built Villa Giulia in 1900, an Art Nouveau hunting lodge whose original style is still admirable today.

The Villa and the old Farmhouse, separated by a secular park, are encircled by 10 hectares of vineyards, growing on a rich calcareous soil with an average elevation of 360 meters.

The estate has been carefully divided to ensure each parcel is left to the most suited varietal.

Varieties grown here: Dolcetto, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc.
