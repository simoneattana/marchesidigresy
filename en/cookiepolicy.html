Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy describes the types of cookies and similar technologies used by the website marchesidigresy.com (the "Site") and the purposes of their use, in accordance with art. 122 of Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 (Privacy Code), Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the guidelines of the Italian Data Protection Authority.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that the websites you visit send to your device, where they are stored to be sent back to the same sites on later visits. Similar technologies (such as localStorage) allow information to be kept in the user's browser for similar purposes.

Cookies may be technical, necessary for the site to work or requested by the user to provide a service, or profiling, aimed at creating user profiles to send advertising in line with their preferences. Technical cookies do not require consent; profiling cookies do.

2. Cookies used by this Site

This Site is built with static technology and takes a privacy-oriented approach. It does not use profiling cookies, nor advertising trackers or statistical analytics.

The only information stored is a technical preference that records the choice made on the cookie banner (accept or decline), so the notice is not shown again on every visit. This information is saved locally on the device (localStorage), is not a profiling cookie and is not shared with third parties.

3. Third-party services

To render typography correctly, the Site loads web fonts from third-party providers:

  • Adobe Fonts (Adobe Inc.) — font delivery; the service does not install profiling cookies.
  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.) — font delivery; the request to the provider's server may involve communicating the user's IP address.

The Site also contains links to external services — WineAround (tasting bookings), Booking Expert (Wine Resort bookings) and Google Maps — which open on third-party sites, each with its own privacy and cookie policy. The Site does not embed social-network widgets or buttons that install cookies.

When the contact form is submitted, the data entered may be transmitted to an external email delivery service, for the sole purpose of receiving and handling the request.

4. Legal basis and consent management

Since the Site only uses technical cookies and technologies, browsing is possible without giving any consent. On the first visit a banner is nonetheless shown, allowing the user to accept or decline; the choice can be changed at any time by clearing the browser's browsing data.

5. How to manage or disable cookies

You can manage your preferences directly from your browser, setting it to block or delete cookies and site data. Below are the guides for the main browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
