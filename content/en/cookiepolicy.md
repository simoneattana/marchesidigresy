sections:
  - title: "1. What cookies are"
    body:
      - >-
        Cookies are small text files that the websites you visit send to your device, where they are
        stored to be sent back to the same sites on later visits. Similar technologies (such as
        <em>localStorage</em>) allow information to be kept in the user's browser for similar purposes.
      - >-
        Cookies may be <strong>technical</strong>, necessary for the site to work or requested by the
        user to provide a service, or <strong>profiling</strong>, aimed at creating user profiles to
        send advertising in line with their preferences. Technical cookies do not require consent;
        profiling cookies do.
  - title: "2. Cookies used by this Site"
    body:
      - >-
        This Site is built with static technology and takes a privacy-oriented approach. It
        <strong>does not use profiling cookies, nor advertising trackers or statistical analytics</strong>.
      - >-
        The only information stored is a technical preference that records the choice made on the cookie
        banner (accept or decline), so the notice is not shown again on every visit. This information is
        saved locally on the device (<em>localStorage</em>), is not a profiling cookie and is not shared
        with third parties.

  - title: "4. Legal basis and consent management"

        clearing the browser's browsing data.
